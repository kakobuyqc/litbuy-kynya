02 / The shopping method

From a saved link
to a considered choice.

A useful Litbuy shopping guide starts with your needs. Keep the product link and your comparison notes together so each decision has a clear basis.

Start browsing CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose the category first

    Start with the gap in your wardrobe: shoes, an everyday T-shirt, a jacket or an accessory. A focused category makes a Litbuy product list easier to review.

  2. Compare the actual listing

    Read the description, inspect available photos and check measurements. Record missing details as unknown rather than filling them in from an inspiration image.

  3. Work out the full budget

    Separate the item price from delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping and potential taxes or duties. An estimate is not a final quote.

  4. Verify before paying

    Confirm seller information, the purchase route and return terms. Where authenticity matters, look for reliable documentation rather than relying on logos or photographs.

03 / Shortlist essentials

What belongs in your spreadsheet?

Use these fields in your own notes. They help separate the look of a product from the information you need to assess it.

Suggested fields for a personal shopping shortlist. This is a checklist, not a live inventory or price feed.
Product categoryDetails to recordUseful comparison
Shoes Model, color, size chart, available measurements and listing link Compare model-specific sizing guidance with your foot measurements.
Hoodies & T-shirts Chest, shoulder, sleeve and length measurements; fabric details Measure a similar garment you own and compare it with the listing.
Jackets & sets Layering space, construction details and separate top/bottom measurements Check whether both pieces of a set match your preferred fit.
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and any stated stretch Use measurements rather than assuming size labels are consistent.
Headwear & jewelry Circumference, dimensions, closure, stated materials and care Confirm physical size and material suitability before choosing.
Every saved item Seller, date checked, quoted price, estimated charges and return terms Keep verified details separate from assumptions and estimates.

A Litbuy clothing spreadsheet or Litbuy shoes spreadsheet can organize research, but a saved row does not verify a seller, guarantee stock or confirm that an item can be purchased through a particular service.

Practical answers about using a Litbuy spreadsheet and navigating this independent category guide.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is generally a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links, categories and comparison notes. The term does not by itself establish who maintains a list or whether any seller, product or purchase route is verified.

Is this an official Litbuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent informational page with links to CAFANS categories. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet, process Litbuy orders or claim an official relationship with Litbuy.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest, shoulder, length and sleeve measurements. For shoes, consult the model-specific size chart and any available foot-length guidance.

Does a product photo prove authenticity or availability?

No. Editorial photos illustrate a style or category and do not confirm CAFANS inventory, seller authorization or authenticity. Verify the actual listing, seller information, purchase terms and supporting documentation before paying.

What costs should I include in a shopping shortlist?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, any applicable service fees, international shipping estimate and potential taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges and check the current purchase terms.

Where do the category links take me?

The category links open the corresponding pages on cafans.com. General browsing buttons open https://www.cafans.com. Product availability and purchase information must be checked on the destination pages.
